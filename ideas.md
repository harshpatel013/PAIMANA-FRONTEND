# Chosen Direction — Civic Ledger

## Design Movement

**Civic Ledger** combines contemporary editorial information design with the rigor of an institutional reporting system. It translates the PAIMANA-AI mission—**find, understand, explain, warn, act**—into a quiet, evidence-first workspace that looks credible to senior government users without feeling bureaucratic or dated.

## Core Principles

1. **Signal before spectacle:** Risk, changes, and recommended review points receive the strongest visual emphasis; charts and decoration remain secondary.
2. **Evidence is legible:** Every metric is accompanied by meaningful context, time references, or a source-oriented label.
3. **Attention is tiered:** Critical signals use measured red accents, high risk uses copper, and routine information stays in ink, stone, and soft blue-grey.
4. **One shared system, role-aware view:** The layout, components, and risk language remain consistent while the content density and navigation adapt by selected demo role.

## Color Philosophy

The foundation uses **paper-white surfaces** over a cool, mist-grey workspace (#F6F8FB), which maintains the government-grade clarity specified in the UI/UX brief. Deep slate ink (#172033) anchors reading and calculation. The supplied soft apricot primary (#FFD9C2) becomes a restrained “review cue” on active selections and intelligence summaries rather than a generic call-to-action color. A muted mineral teal gives interactive elements institutional authority. Risk colors stay semantic and local: green for low, amber for moderate, copper for high, and red only for critical exceptions.

## Layout Paradigm

The interface behaves like an **open operational ledger** rather than a centered dashboard. A narrow fixed rail offers wayfinding. The content canvas begins with an asymmetrical title and date area, then opens into modular evidence plates: a broad analytic field paired with a compact decision column. Project intelligence is structured as a chronological investigative narrative rather than a uniform card grid.

## Signature Elements

1. **Ledger stripes:** a 4px risk-colored vertical rule and micro-label at the start of high-priority evidence plates.
2. **Data stamps:** small uppercase labels and framed last-updated text that make each metric feel auditable.
3. **Contour field:** a low-contrast, abstract infrastructure/survey pattern in non-functional large surfaces, used once as a branded visual anchor rather than repeatedly.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like reviewing an intelligence dossier. Filtering, role selection, tabs, alerts, and table rows react immediately with clear state changes. Clicking a critical project preserves the investigation context and reveals the project dossier in place. The interface does not simulate decisions; it frames findings as **recommended areas for review**.

## Animation

Motion is restrained, purposeful, and under 220ms. Evidence plates lift by 1–2px on hover; active filters and role choices use a quick color/outline transition; page modules enter with a short 40ms stagger and opacity/translate only. Critical indicators never pulse continuously. All non-essential animation is disabled under reduced-motion preferences.

## Typography System

**Manrope** provides a contemporary, highly readable UI voice for headings, navigation, and body copy. **IBM Plex Mono** is reserved for project identifiers, dates, values, and metadata to reinforce the evidence-led character. Display hierarchy uses 30–34px semi-bold page titles, 18–20px section headings, 13px uppercase tracking for data stamps, and 13–15px body copy. Numerical KPIs use tabular figures and avoid oversized decorative numerals.

## Brand Essence

**PAIMANA-AI is the evidence-led national infrastructure intelligence workspace for public-sector teams that need to surface risk early and focus review where it matters.**

**Personality:** Measured, capable, public-minded.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and observational. Calls to action invite inspection instead of overselling certainty. Microcopy distinguishes prediction from decision and keeps terminology plain.

> “63 projects merit immediate review.”

> “Inspect the contributing conditions before escalating.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The logo mark is a bold, text-free **stacked survey beacon**: three offset, rounded-edged survey lines converge into one upward focal point, suggesting infrastructure corridors, measured progress, and a decision signal. The wordmark combines the PAIMANA-AI name in Manrope semi-bold with the mark at a clearly visible size.

## Signature Brand Color

**PAIMANA Mineral Teal — #0B695C.**

## Style Decisions

- The stacked survey beacon and PAIMANA-AI wordmark appear in both the persistent workspace rail and the global top bar, ensuring every authenticated view is visibly part of one civic-intelligence system.
- Mineral Teal (#0B695C) is the only primary action color. Blue remains outside the product action palette.
- High-priority evidence uses an accent ledger stripe and uppercase mono evidence stamp before relying on card shadows, large scores, or status badges.
